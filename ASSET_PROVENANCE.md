# Concept-image provenance

The four images in `source-images/` were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project, then converted to WebP in `dist/assets/`. They are illustrative architectural concepts. They do not depict Steven West's actual projects. Visible labels and alt text on the site make that distinction clear.

| Asset | Final prompt |
| --- | --- |
| `concept-exterior` | Editorial website hero placeholder: original photograph-like image of a grand but warm contemporary classical residence, pale limestone and brick, mature trees, late-afternoon light, wide layout with darker garden space on the left for a headline. No people, logos, signage, text or watermark. |
| `concept-staircase` | Editorial portfolio placeholder: original warm residence interior with a sculptural curved staircase in pale stone and plaster, dark oak handrail and soft daylight; vertical architecture-magazine composition. No people, text, logos or watermark. |
| `concept-living-room` | Editorial portfolio placeholder: original spacious living room with pale plaster arches, limestone fireplace, dark timber beams, warm linen furnishings, understated art and daylight; wide architecture-magazine composition. No people, text, logos or watermark. |
| `concept-kitchen` | Editorial portfolio placeholder: original handcrafted kitchen with warm white millwork, honed marble island, aged brass accents, dark oak floor and garden-facing daylight; wide architecture-magazine composition. No people, text, logos or watermark. |

All prompts specified that the output was an illustrative concept rather than a specific real Steven West project. The built-in tool mode was used. Each source and WebP final is saved inside this project folder.
